  • 一个面板管理多个账号

    Manage multiple accounts in one panel

    • 每个账号一列，同时看到、同时使用
    • 登录互相隔离，只需登录一次，重启也不掉线
    • 账号名居中大字显示，点 ✎ 即可改名
    • 每周额度进度条、重置日期和不过期额度一眼看清
    • ⚙ 菜单：刷新、休眠以释放内存、删除
    • One column per account, all visible and usable at once
    • Separate logins: sign in once and stay signed in across restarts
    • Account names shown large and centered; click ✎ to rename
    • Weekly quota bar, reset date and non-expiring quota at a glance
    • ⚙ menu: reload, sleep to free memory, or remove

  • 方便地分组、编辑组

    Group and edit groups easily

    • 左侧分组栏，点击即可切换
    • 右键组名即可编辑或删除，删除前会确认
    • 拖动组名排序；把列头拖到组名上即可移组
    • 每个组有固定颜色的竖条，一眼区分
    • 分组栏可拖宽，也能收起成一条细条
    • Group sidebar on the left; click a group to switch
    • Right-click a group to rename or delete it, with confirmation
    • Drag groups to reorder; drop a column header on a group to move the account
    • A fixed color bar on each group makes them easy to tell apart
    • Resize the sidebar or collapse it into a thin strip

第一次打开

First launch

  1. 打开下载的 .dmg，把 Muse Panel 拖进「应用程序」。
  2. 安装包未经 Apple 公证：在 Finder 里右键 Muse Panel →「打开」，再点「打开」；或到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」点「仍要打开」。
  3. 也可以在终端执行：xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Muse Panel.app"
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Muse Panel into Applications.
  2. The app isn't notarized by Apple: right-click Muse Panel in Finder → Open and confirm, or use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Or run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Muse Panel.app"
